The Night Bus

Paragraf numaraları solda.

1

At three o'clock in the morning, most people in the city are asleep. Nadia Brooks isn't. She is a bus driver, and she drives the N7 night bus from the city centre to the airport and back again. She starts work at eleven o'clock at night and finishes at seven in the morning. "People think the night is quiet," she says, "but my bus is never empty."

2

Her passengers are very different. Some are nurses, cleaners and cooks. They finish work late or start very early, so the night bus is the only way for them to get to work or go home. Others are students after a party, or travellers with big suitcases on their way to catch a plane. Nadia always has two small bags of sweets next to her seat. One bag is for the travellers' children, and the other is for the night workers. "The workers love them3 more than the children!" she laughs.

3

The N7 is twelve years old, and in winter the heating sometimes doesn't work. But the bus is always spotless4. Nadia cleans the seats and the floor before every journey, and you never see any rubbish. Sometimes a passenger falls asleep and misses their stop. Nadia never gets angry about it5. When the bus gets to the airport, she wakes the passenger up and tells them the time of the next bus back.

4

Many passengers are grateful6. They often say "Thank you, Nadia!" when they get off, and an old man called Mr Patel sometimes brings her a cup of coffee from the airport café. Nadia knows a lot of her passengers' names, and they know hers.

5

Nadia lives with her eleven-year-old daughter, Lily, and her mother, Rosa. When Nadia gets home at half past seven, Rosa is always in the kitchen and Lily is in her bedroom with her school books. They have breakfast together, and then Nadia goes to bed. She8 is usually asleep before Lily leaves for school.

6

"My friends often ask me, 'Why don't you get a day job?'" says Nadia. "But I like my hours, and I love the people on my bus. They are my night family."
