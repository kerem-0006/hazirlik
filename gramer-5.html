1

Sıfatın yeri · look like

Sıfat Türkçedeki gibi ismin önüne gelir: a young man (genç bir adam).

KuralÖrnek
sıfat + isimHe's a young man. · She's got long hair.
be / look + sıfatHe's tall. · She looks young.
quite / very + sıfatShe's quite young. · He's very tall.

Görünüş sorusu: What does he look like? = Nasıl görünüyor? Cevap: He's tall. He's got grey hair.

Karışmasın: What does he like? = Neyi sever?

2

was · were

am / is / are'nin geçmiş hâli: "-dı, -di". The party was great = Parti harikaydı.

KimOlumluOlumsuzSoru
I · he · she · itwaswasn'tWas it good?
you · we · theywereweren'tWere they nice?

Kısa cevap: Was it good? Yes, it was. · Were they late? No, they weren't.

Soru kelimesiyle: How many people were there? · Why was it terrible?

Kolay yol: am ve is → was, are → were.

3

can · can't

Yapabildiğin ve yapamadığın şeyler: "-ebilmek". I can swim = Yüzebilirim.

KalıpÖrnek
+can + yalın fiilI can speak two languages.
−can't + yalın fiilI can't cook very well.
?Can + özne + fiilCan you play chess? Yes, I can. · No, I can't.
iyican + fiil + really wellHe can speak Italian really well.

Üç sık hata: he/she/it ile de can ("cans" yok). Soruda do yok: Can you...? ("Do you can" değil). can'den sonra yalın fiil: can't play ("playing" değil).
