1

have got · sahip olmak

Türkçedeki "-m var". I've got a laptop. = Bir laptopum var.

Tablo

Kim+ olumlu− olumsuz? soru
I / you / we / theyhave got ('ve got)haven't gotHave ... got?
he / she / ithas got ('s got)hasn't gotHas ... got?

Tek kural

he / she / it → has · gerisi → have

Dikkat: He's got içindeki 's = has, "is" değil.

Kısa cevapta got yok

Have you got a laptop?

→ Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.

a / any

tek şey → a / an: I've got a car.

olumsuz ve soruda çoğul → any: I haven't got any sunglasses. (s.118)

Sık hata

He have got a car. → He has got a car.

Yes, I have got. → Yes, I have.

2

Geniş zaman: he / she / it + s

Kitaptan: I get up at 7. → Keira gets up at 2 a.m.

Tek kural

he / she / it → fiil + s

I / you / we / they → fiil olduğu gibi

Olumsuz

doesn't + yalın fiil

She doesn't like her job. · He doesn't have lunch.

doesn't likes: -s bir kere gelir.

Yazım (s.25)

Fiil nasıl bitiyorEkKitabın örneği
normal-sShe loves her job.
-s, -sh, -ch, -x, -z, -o-esHe finishes work at 7 a.m. · He goes to bed.
sessiz harf + yy → -iesHe worries about the traffic.
havehasHe has lunch at 1 p.m.

Sık hata

He is goes to bed. → He goes to bed. Geniş zamanda is / are yok.

He gos to work. → He goes to work.

Alıştırma · fiili doğru yaz

1
He TV every evening. (watch)
2
She English at university. (study)
3
My dad to work at 8. (go)
4
The film at 9 p.m. (start)
5
Ali football on Sundays. (play)
6
He breakfast at 7. (have)
7
She coffee. (not like)
8
We TV in the morning. (not watch)

İpucu: 5'te play'in sonundaki y'den önce sesli harf var. 8'de özne we, he/she/it değil.

3

Dükkânda alışveriş

Here you are = "Buyurun". Birine bir şey uzatırken söylenir, kelime kelime çevrilmez.

MüşteriHave you got any water?
SatıcıYes. How many bottles would you like?
MüşteriTwo, please.
Satıcışişeleri uzatıyorHere you are. Anything else?
MüşteriNo, thanks. How much is that?
SatıcıThat's £1.25, please. Cash or card?
MüşteriCan I pay by card?

Kalıplar kitabın s.20 Useful phrases kutusundan, diyalog örnek olarak kuruldu.
